Overview

ACAAN is a performance tool for close-up card magic, built around the classic "Any Card At Any Number" plot. The phone stands in for a real deck: the performer secretly sets a target card, hands the device to a spectator, and whatever they do with the cards, the first one they turn over is the target.

The secret is in the deck graphic itself. The closed deck hides an invisible 2×2 grid — one quadrant per suit — and the open deck an invisible 3×5 grid running from Ace to King. Two taps that read as nothing more than handling the deck arm the effect, with no menus, no typing and no tells.

Once the deck is spread, all 52 cards become individually draggable and flippable objects. The spectator can shuffle them around the screen, stack them and browse as long as they like; the force is applied at the first flip.

A performance, start to finish

Choose the suit with one tap on the closed deck, choose the value with a second tap on the open deck, and hand the phone over. The cards spread into a loose pile, each with a small random offset and tilt, and every touched card lifts, scales and comes to the front.

The first flip turns the target card with a 3D rotation. One button gathers the cards back and reshuffles a fresh deck for the next spectator.

What it does

Invisible controls

  • Suit and rank are picked by touching the deck image itself
  • Two exit modes: tap to leave, or a single swipe that picks the rank and slides the deck away
  • The force stays consistent even if every remaining card is turned over

Card themes

  • 38 decks and card backs, browsed in a scaling carousel
  • New themes are just images plus a manifest entry
  • The chosen theme is remembered

Tunable handling

  • Flip with a single tap for speed or a double tap for safety
  • Flip duration adjustable from half a second to five seconds

Physical feel

  • Perspective 3D flip with overshoot and scale
  • Cards lift on drag with shadows that follow their height above the table

Built for the stage

  • Bilingual illustrated guide in Italian and English
  • Portrait lock, full-screen layout and pre-cached images so nothing pops in mid-show

How it is built

ACAAN is a Flutter and Dart app with a single codebase for Android and iOS, running entirely offline from bundled assets. Selection grids are computed from the geometry of the deck images, themes are described in a JSON manifest, and settings are persisted on the device. A small native Android component adapts the full-screen mode to the device's navigation style.

Tech stack

Dart

Drives the deck state machine (inDeck → deckOpening → open → scrollingOut → gathering), the hit-testing that turns a touch Offset into a suit or rank, the face-swap that applies the force, and the list reordering used as a z-index for card stacking.

Flutter

Renders the splash screen, deck and 52 card widgets.

JSON theme manifest

Card themes are described in a small bundled manifest, so adding a deck means adding images and one entry.

shared_preferences

Persists the performer's setup — the selected card theme and the deck exit mode — so the app reopens configured exactly as it was left.

Kotlin platform channel

A navigation_mode_channel MethodChannel reports whether the Android device uses gesture or three-button navigation, so the app picks the immersive system-UI mode that keeps the deck full-screen without the system bars intruding.
