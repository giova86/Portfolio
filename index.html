About me

Data Scientist specializing in industrial analytics, machine learning, and production optimization. I design and deploy data-driven solutions that turn complex industrial data into real-time insights, predictive capabilities, and measurable business value. My expertise lies in building robust machine learning models that forecast equipment behavior, detect anomalies, and optimize production workflows. Beyond structured data, I integrate large language models (LLMs) to process and analyze unstructured industrial information—such as maintenance logs, shift reports, and technical documentation. Whether improving operational efficiency, reducing unplanned downtime, or enabling smarter decision-making on the shop floor, I’m passionate about using data and AI to drive digital transformation in industry.
